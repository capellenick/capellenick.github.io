About Nick Capelle

01Beginnings

My love for design began with drawing and illustration. As a kid, I would constantly draw Spider-Man, Tom and Jerry, Master Chief, or my own name. Over time, I explored more mediums such as painting, ink, graphite, and charcoal while paying more attention to the art world around me. To this day, visiting art museums is one of my favorite things to do!

02Noticing

Throughout high school, I grew a fascination with computer science and the intersection of technology and design. I started delving into the world of product design and began noticing all the subpar UX around me, whether that was autoplay on Netflix or YouTube, a useful button removed from an interface, or even an unclear door handle. From then on, I knew I wanted to help design a more intuitive, user-friendly world.

03Process

My practice is driven by curiosity and play. I like to break things down to their basic elements, often through modular systems, math, and science, and rebuild them toward something unexpected. I’m drawn to opposites: the tactile and the digital, the natural and the systematic, the everyday and the surprising. Much of my work asks you to take part, inviting small, low-pressure moments of curiosity, presence, and play.
